Tickr and PocketScribe

Two small personal apps that use Google sign-in to keep their data in the user's own Google Drive.

Tickr

A Mac app for stock watchlists, charts and price alerts. Signing in with Google lets Tickr keep watchlists, alerts and settings in a private app-data folder in the user's own Google Drive, so every Mac they sign in to shows the same lists. Tickr can't see any other files in Drive.

PocketScribe

An Android voice recorder. Connecting Google Drive lets PocketScribe upload the recordings it makes to the user's own Google Drive. It can only see the files it created.

Neither app has a server. Data goes straight between the user's device and their Google account.
