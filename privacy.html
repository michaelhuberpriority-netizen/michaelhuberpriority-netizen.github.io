Privacy policy

Tickr and PocketScribe · Last updated November 2, 2026

What the apps access

  • Your Google account's email address, name and profile picture, to show which account is signed in.
  • Tickr: its own app-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). Tickr stores your watchlists, price alerts and settings there. It can't see any other files in your Drive.
  • PocketScribe: only the files it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission), which are your recordings.

Where your data goes

Data moves only between your device and your own Google account. The apps have no servers, collect no analytics, and don't sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Sign-in tokens stay on your device.

Google API Services

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Sign out in the app, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Tickr's synced data is hidden app data in your Drive. Delete it in Google Drive under Settings › Manage apps › Options › Delete hidden app data. Tickr also keeps a local copy in ~/Library/Application Support/Tickr on each Mac; deleting that folder removes it.
  • PocketScribe recordings are ordinary files in your Drive; delete them like any other file. Uninstalling PocketScribe removes the copies on your phone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions can go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for these apps.
